Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is it a standard, off-the-shelf system?

No. We start from a proven system and tailor it to your needs: your products, your rules and the reports you rely on.

Can I see the system before I decide?

Yes. Book a demo and we will walk you through it using examples from your business.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Pay a 30% deposit to start, then spread the rest over 3 to 6 months by mobile money or bank.

Does Eltech POS work without internet?

Yes. Keep selling offline. Your sales sync automatically when the connection returns.

Can I use Eltech Finance on my phone?

Yes. It installs on your phone like an app, so you can check on your business from anywhere.

Does payroll handle PAYE and NSSF?

Yes. Eltech Finance calculates PAYE and NSSF as part of payroll.

What happens after we go live?

We stay with you. Reach our Kampala team by phone, WhatsApp or email.
