F.A.Q

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how we work with clients and deliver IT outcomes.

01

Do you work with our existing IT team or replace them?

Both. We often augment internal IT with specialized delivery (development, cloud, security) and clear runbooks. If you need fully managed operations, we can take day-to-day ownership under agreed SLAs.

02

Which cloud platforms and stacks do you support?

We work across Microsoft 365/Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, and hybrid environments. Applications are commonly .NET, Node, Python, or modern JavaScript frameworks, with mobile in Flutter or native stacks—always matched to your constraints.

03

How do you handle security and data privacy?

Security is embedded in design: least-privilege access, encryption, secure SDLC practices, and logging. We align deliverables to your policies and can support GDPR-style and sector-specific requirements where applicable.

04

What does a typical project timeline look like?

After discovery (1–3 weeks for midsize efforts), we propose milestones: design sign-off, build sprints, UAT, and go-live with hypercare. Complex integrations or migrations may run longer; you always get a written plan before build starts.

05

Can you maintain what you build?

Yes. Most clients choose a managed services retainer or a defined support window after launch. We document environments and provide escalation paths so you are never dependent on a single individual.
